## ABSTRACT

This talk presents an in-house developed piezoelectric-MEMS vibration sensor module and its usage in smart manufacturing where Case (i) surface roughness prediction of fused silica substrate in a CNC polishing machine, Case (ii) fault diagnosis of a rolling bearing in an electric motor through their vibration signatures and machine learning (ML), and Case (iii) lifetime prediction of a drilling system are implemented as successful embodiments. In such a vibration sensing module, both PZT and AlN thin-film piezoelectric materials were verified while the differential configuration of the MEMS vibration sensor offers enhanced acceleration sensitivity for out-of-plane motion with common-mode noise rejection capability, therefore leading to high signal to noise ratio. The Thin-film Piezo on Silicon (TPoS) vibration sensor with a piezoelectric layer was fabricated using photolithography, frontal etching, and backside release. A fully integrated module comprising MEMS accelerometer, analog front-end, and a microcontroller was designed and characterized in a shaker system. The designed module provides a sensitivity of&gt;100 mV/g, a resolution of&lt;1mg/√ Hz, and a bandwidth of 5kHz. The developed module features excellent compatibility with industrial 4.0 intelligent manufacturing applications.
