- **ArCats (Ar _chitecture Refactoring and Compositional Analysis Tool Suite)_**

ArCats is a concurrency analysis tool which is enhanced by architecture refactoring to increase the scalability of compositional analysis.\
Its front-end language is a subset of Promela enhanced with architecture refactoring.\
Links: [ArCats.htm](http://staff.csie.ncu.edu.tw/ypcheng/ArCats.htm)
